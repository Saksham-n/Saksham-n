# Saksham

I like difficult problems.

Most of my repositories start with a simple question:

> *"Can I build this?"*

Sometimes they fail.

Sometimes they become something I'm proud of.

Either way, I leave knowing more than I did yesterday.

- Building products with React, Node.js and PostgreSQL.
- Exploring AI beyond chatbots.
- Learning distributed systems one bug at a time.

---

### Philosophy

I don't believe great engineers are the ones who know every framework.

They're the ones who stay curious long enough to build something worthwhile.
